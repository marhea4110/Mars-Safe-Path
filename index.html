02 / TECHNICAL APPROACH

How the prototype works

01

Visualise the region

Leaflet displays NASA Mars Trek map tiles. Users can compare visible imagery, global elevation shading and daytime infrared imagery.

02

Model traversal cost

A small 16 × 16 synthetic grid assigns different movement costs to cells. Higher values represent hypothetical difficult terrain in this demonstration only.

03

Search with A*

A* uses accumulated movement cost plus a distance heuristic to find a lower-cost path through the synthetic grid. A direct line is shown as a baseline.

04 / LIMITATIONS & NEXT SCIENTIFIC STEP

A transparent research prototype.

  • The A* cost grid and circular zones are invented demonstration values; they are not extracted from NASA images or terrain rasters.
  • The NASA layers are visual context only. This version does not perform pixel classification, DEM slope calculation, thermal analysis or automated hazard detection.
  • Global MOLA and THEMIS products have resolutions and measurement properties that are not suitable for detecting every astronaut-scale obstacle.
  • Distances are approximate map estimates. The model does not include rover/astronaut mobility, power, communications, radiation forecasts, weather or operational constraints.
  • This student prototype is for education and demonstration only. It must not be used for real mission operations or safety decisions.
